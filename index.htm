Welcome to Apo Island

Apo Island is a beautiful volcanic island located off the coast of Dauin in Negros Oriental, Philippines. It is known for its clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and abundant marine life.

The island is especially famous for its sea turtles. Snorkeling and diving around its protected marine sanctuary offer visitors the chance to see turtles, colorful fish, and beautiful coral gardens in their natural environment.

Beyond the underwater scenery, Apo Island offers a peaceful escape where visitors can enjoy its beaches, explore the island, meet the local community, and experience the simple beauty of island life.

What to Know?

Everything you need to know before visiting the island.

Island Facilities

The island operates on a cash-only basis with no ATMs. Electricity is typically limited to specific evening hours.

Sanctuary Rules

Touching or chasing marine life is strictly prohibited. Visitors must use a local guide and pay the environmental fee to enter the turtle zone.

Weather Factors

Mornings offer the calmest sea crossings, making them ideal for traveling to the island.
